**Chapter 8核心：企業不只要把資安做好，還要符合哪些外部要求？**  

---

**最重要的兩條線**  

1. 法遵主線  
   ```text
    Applicable Requirement
            ↓
    Compliance
            ↓
    Control
            ↓
    Implementation
            ↓
    Evidence
            ↓
    Audit
            ↓
    Gap
            ↓
    Corrective Action
            ↓
    Continuous Improvement
   ```
2. 資安事件主線
   ```text
    Security Incident
            ↓
    Impact Assessment
            ↓
    Privacy / Compliance Assessment
            ↓
    Notification / Reporting
            ↓
    Legal Responsibility
            ↓
    Remediation
            ↓
    Lessons Learned
            ↓
    Continuous Improvement
   ```
